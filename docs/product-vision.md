# TechRadar - Product Vision

## The Idea

After a long job search, I realized that while I had spent years studying Computer Science, there was another part of the tech world I wasn't following nearly enough: the everyday conversation around it.

I know who's dating whom, what happened on the latest reality show, and probably a little too much celebrity gossip, so why wasn't I just as up to date on the industry I actually wanted to work in?

So I decided to build my own Ynet for tech.

TechRadar is a personal platform designed to help me continuously explore the technology industry while improving as a software engineer.

## What I Want to Follow

TechRadar focuses on 4 areas:

### Tech Feed
Important developments in technology, established companies, products, AI/ML, software engineering and infrastructure.

Instead of simply showing articles, TechRadar should help answer:

- What happened?
- Why should I care?
- What is technically interesting about it?
- Where can I read more?

### Startup Radar
Discover interesting startups while they are still being built and follow how they evolve over time.

### Tech Knowledge
Short explanations of Computer Science and software engineering concepts I want to understand better.

Examples include:

- CI/CD
- Docker
- Kubernetes
- Redis
- Kafka
- Load Balancers
- Observability
- Distributed Systems
- Vector Databases
- RAG
- AI Agents

The goal is to understand not only what these technologies are, but what problems they solve and how they are used in real systems.

### Daily Coding
A daily coding challenge to keep practicing algorithms and problem solving while preparing for software engineering interviews.

## Product Philosophy

TechRadar should not become another infinite feed.

The goal is not to consume as much information as possible.

Each day, TechRadar should create a small, curated Daily Edition containing the things that are actually worth knowing.

I should be able to open TechRadar, spend around 10–15 minutes learning, and leave knowing more about the industry than I did before.

## Engineering Goal

TechRadar is also a learning project.

The goal is to build a real software system while exploring:

- Backend development
- APIs
- Databases
- Data ingestion
- Background jobs
- LLM integration
- Ranking and recommendation
- Testing
- CI/CD
- Cloud deployment
- Observability

AI should enhance the system, not replace the engineering behind it.
